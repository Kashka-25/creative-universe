A tour of Kronk

A small community, built together.

Kronk is a community-owned social platform. No ads, no algorithm deciding what you see, no tracking. Every relationship is mutual, and the members decide how it runs.

This tour walks through it with made-up members and music, so you can click around without an account.

Use Next at the bottom, the steps at the top, or your arrow keys.
What you'll see
Everything here is example data, kept in this page only.
Home

Your feed, in order, from your people.

Home shows posts from your Mates and the spaces you've tuned in to, newest first. Nothing is ranked or boosted.

Froth a post to show some love. Only the person who posted sees who frothed.

Try it: froth a post.

Mates and reach

Mutual by design. You choose how far a post goes.

There are no followers. A Mate is someone who asked and was accepted, both ways.

Every post sits on one reach ladder: Just me, Mates, Orbit (Mates of Mates) or Kronkverse (the whole community).

Try it: pick a reach, or send a Mate request.

The Hub

Korners: spaces the community builds.

The Hub holds Kronk's korners: a calendar, open governance, questions, moments, a marketplace, and the Booth for music.

A korner only reaches your feed when you tune in to it.

Try it: tune in to a korner, or open the Booth.

The Booth

A stage for music made by the people around you.

DJ sets, mixes, tracks, albums, readings and live sets, played and credited to whoever made them.

First listens puts the sets with the fewest plays first, so new voices get heard before the familiar ones.

Try it: play a set, ♡ save one, then switch to My sets or First listens.

One set

Credits, a track list, and Shoutouts.

Each set has its own page. Collaborators confirm their credit, and the set then shows under their name too.

Shoutouts are short notes to the artist, kept on the set where everyone listening can see them.

Try it: leave a Shoutout, or tap a time to jump.
Live sets

Go live now, or at a time you set.

Members listen in together from Live. Coming-up sets show with a Remind me so you get a nudge when they start.

Try it: press Remind me.

Wachuneed

Wachuneed? Wachugot?

Members offer art, stuff and services to each other, and post what they're looking for. Trust comes from being in the same community.

Kronk takes no cut and handles no payment: you message the person in Nudges and sort it out between you.

Try it: switch between Offered and Desired.

Join Kronk

Kronk grows by invitation.

There's no open sign-up. Everyone on Kronk was invited by someone already there, and every member can invite the next person.

If you know someone on Kronk, ask them for an invite link: it opens the sign-up page. If not, start with the About page.

On the way in, every member says
I claim ownership of my Kronk experience.
Reach, feed and spaces are yours to set.
I am a custodian of the Kronk community.
Invite with care; look after the people here.
I share responsibility for the trajectory of Kronk.
Changes are proposed and decided in the open, in Kommons.
